Our studio

Where our story began, and yours will shine.

At Rajas Ink, tattooing is treated as a craft, a ritual, and a form of storytelling. Located in the heart of Ubud, our studio blends contemporary tattoo culture with the deep artistic roots of Bali. Every line, shadow, and symbol is created with intention, respect, and technical precision.

Our approach

Friendly space, professional craft.

Our space is designed to be friendly for you to feel at home, and professional for your art to be unique. We work exclusively with experienced Balinese tattoo artists who combine traditional discipline with modern techniques. Hygiene, comfort, and artistic integrity are central to everything we do — from the first consultation to the final result.

Good to know

FAQ

Pain levels vary depending on placement, size, and individual tolerance. Most people describe the sensation as uncomfortable but manageable. Our artists work calmly and efficiently to make the experience as smooth as possible.

Tattoo prices depend on size, detail, placement, and time required. We provide clear pricing after a consultation, ensuring transparency and no surprises before starting the work.

Proper aftercare is essential. You'll receive detailed instructions, including cleaning, moisturizing, and sun protection. Following these steps helps prevent infection and ensures the best healing and long-term results.

Initial healing usually takes 2–4 weeks, while complete healing beneath the skin can take longer. During this time, it's important to avoid sun exposure, swimming, and excessive friction.

You can book by contacting us directly through WhatsApp or Instagram. Share your idea, reference images, and preferred size or placement, and we'll guide you through the next steps.

Check the gallery above for a sense of our range — from fine line and dotwork to bold traditional Balinese pieces. Bring your reference images and we'll talk through what's possible.
